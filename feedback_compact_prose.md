In design discussions, be more compact: "Less connective wordage. Same
sense." (2026-07-12, during the handoff compaction discussion.)

**Why:** Connective tissue (transitions, framing, restated context)
costs reading time without adding content; ddaa reads fast and challenges
substance directly.

**How to apply:** State claims and their grounds directly; drop
transitional sentences, hedged framings, and re-summaries of what was
just said. Compactness is cutting connectives, not dropping content —
completeness of substance stays. See [[feedback-systemmessage-style]]
for the same instinct applied to hook output.
